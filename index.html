A FEW POINTERS

Make it your way.

Move with WASD / arrow keys. Stand near a station and hold E until the ring fills. Use your cursor for the task at that station.

Clay supply · a full clumpHold E at the supply crates and collect clay. Every design starts from the same large, unshaped clump.

Rough shaping · push clayAt the shaping station, drag an outer edge toward the middle to push clay together. The broad hand tool makes rough forms; it cannot cut or paint.

Carving bench · cut clayAt the carving bench, drag the chisel to remove clay. Use a small tool for fine details. Undo mistakes before leaving this station.

Painting table · paint clayChoose a color and brush it directly onto the surface. Painting only changes color. You can visit the stations in any order.

Collection counter · get a scoreSubmit your work at Check & deliver. A score of 90% or more passes, pays coins and unlocks the next reference. Below 90% restarts the level; collect a new clump to try again.

Compare your sculpture by eye with the separate reference picture. Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes this station's last stroke; [ and ] resize the tool. Firing at the kiln is optional. Your current attempt is saved in this tab.
